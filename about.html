OUR STORY

Welcome to the Misty Hills

Lambasingi is a scenic hill destination surrounded by green forests, mountains and peaceful valleys.

The cool climate and misty landscape attract travellers looking for a peaceful escape from busy city life.

Visitors can enjoy nature, agriculture, local culture, village life and scenic mountain views.
